Mirror check

What Malaysia says it exported to each partner, against what that partner says it imported from Malaysia. Partners record imports by origin and value them CIF; Malaysia records exports by destination, FOB. Hong Kong and Singapore re-export, so their gaps are structural. Totals are compared by weight and by value, because some partners file a value without a weight. Months either side has not released are left blank.
